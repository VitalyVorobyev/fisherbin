# START_HERE — cold-start note for a new ScoreQuant research agent

Read `README.md` (the canonical read order), then `PROBLEM.md` and
`AGENT.md`, then your `WORK/active/` packet.

**Only if you are a genuinely fresh agent** (first contact with this
workspace, no packet assigned), reply first with a short scope check — a
paragraph restating the canonical problem, the three problem levels, the
full-D / in-bin \(D_s\) / projected efficient-score distinction, and why a
finite \(D_s\) optimum is not automatically deployable while a terminal D
exchange solution is. Do not repeat this ritual on subsequent sessions.

Before selecting a target, use `CLAIMS.json` as a dependency graph and list
the unresolved nodes blocking it. Do not attempt a theorem outside a work
packet without recording one in `WORK/active/`.
